🦐 Neocaridina Shrimp Care Guide

  • Water Temperature: 18°C – 24°C (65°F – 75°F)
  • pH Levels: 6.5 – 8.0 (neutral to slightly alkaline preferred)
  • Hardness: GH 4–8, KH 3–15 for healthy molting and shell growth
  • Tank Size: Minimum 10 liters (fully cycled with a sponge filter)
  • Diet: High-quality shrimp pellets, blanched vegetables (spinach/zucchini), and natural biofilm/algae
  • Tank Mates: Peaceful tank mates like small snails or small nano fish to prevent predation
