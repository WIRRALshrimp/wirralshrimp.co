ABOUT

A little shrimp shop from the Wirral.

Wirral Shrimp Co. is a small freshwater shrimp project focused on healthy livestock and good aquarium keeping.

  • 💧 Water quality: Simple guidance for stable aquarium conditions.

SHRIMP CARE

Keep them happy.

  • 01 — Cycle: Never add shrimp to an uncycled aquarium.
  • 02 — Stability: Keep water conditions consistent.
  • 03 — Cover: Moss, plants and hiding places make great habitat.
